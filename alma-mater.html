Four institutions that shaped the path from school foundations in India to graduate research in safe autonomous systems.

School

DPS Duliajan

A formative school foundation in Assam, with a CBSE academic environment that emphasizes critical thinking, co-curricular growth, arts, sports, and leadership.

Visit DPS Duliajan
School

DPS Jodhpur

Delhi Public School Jodhpur continued the school journey with a disciplined academic culture, broad senior-secondary streams, and a strong co-curricular learning environment.

Visit DPS Jodhpur
Undergraduate

BITS Pilani

Birla Institute of Technology and Science, Pilani, was the undergraduate home for engineering, innovation, and early research growth before the move into autonomous systems at CMU.

Visit BITS Pilani
Doctoral

Carnegie Mellon University

Carnegie Mellon University is the current Ph.D. home for work in Electrical and Computer Engineering, CyLab, CADRE, and safe autonomous vehicle research.
