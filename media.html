CMU Researchers Share Work with Secretary of Transportation Pete Buttigieg

CMU covered Secretary Pete Buttigieg's visit to Mill 19, where Safety21 researchers demonstrated transportation innovations. The article notes that Nishad Sahu, Gregory Su, and Raj Rajkumar demonstrated safe navigation of autonomous driving systems in designated work zones using high-definition mapping, computer perception, and vehicle connectivity.
