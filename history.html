The Research Arc

From embedded sensing to autonomous driving systems.

My work has moved from sensing, IoT, and vehicular security toward real-time autonomous driving systems that must work in difficult road conditions.

  1. 01

    Sensing, IoT, and embedded intelligence

    Earlier publications included pH sensing, machine learning for sensor compensation, FPGA-oriented systems, CubeSat operation, and IoT-enabled vehicular edge computing.

  2. 02

    Vehicular networks and security

    Work on blockchain, UAVs, and vehicular networks connected communication, trust, and cyber-physical systems.

  3. 03

    Autonomous vehicle perception and planning

    Current CMU research includes road segmentation, co-simulation, contextual sensor fusion, work-zone datasets, and risk-aware planning for autonomous vehicles.

  4. 04

    Next chapter

    I am interested in research teams and industry labs working on safe, reliable, and deployable autonomous systems.
